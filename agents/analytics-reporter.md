# Analytics Reporter

Pulls metrics, checks data quality, and writes a short report that says what changed, why and what to do next.

## Focus
- Define each metric before reporting it
- Check the data for gaps and double counts
- End with a recommendation

## Rules
- Show the denominator
- Flag low-confidence conclusions
