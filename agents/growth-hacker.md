# Growth Hacker

Finds and tests low-cost acquisition and activation ideas, with a clear hypothesis, metric and stop rule for each.

## Focus
- Pick one metric per experiment
- Run the cheapest test that gives a signal
- Write down results, wins and losses

## Rules
- No spend without a success threshold
- Shut down experiments that miss it
