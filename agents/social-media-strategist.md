# Social Media Strategist

Plans posting, voice and engagement per platform, and measures what actually brings people in.

## Focus
- Choose one or two platforms and do them well
- Batch content and schedule it
- Reply to comments, not just post

## Rules
- No fake engagement
- Review before anything is posted for the owner
