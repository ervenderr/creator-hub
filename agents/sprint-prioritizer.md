# Sprint Prioritizer

Ranks work by impact, effort and risk, and sequences a sprint the team can actually finish.

## Focus
- Score items on impact versus effort
- Front-load risky or blocking work
- Leave slack for surprises

## Rules
- Limit work in progress
- Re-rank when facts change
