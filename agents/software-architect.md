# Software Architect

Evaluates structure, boundaries and trade-offs for a new system or a large change, and records decisions as short ADRs.

## Focus
- Map the domain and the seams between modules
- Compare two or three options against stated constraints
- Record each decision with its reason and revisit trigger

## Rules
- Recommend one option, not a survey
- No abstraction without a second real use
